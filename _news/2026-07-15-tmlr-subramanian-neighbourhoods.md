We are thrilled to announce that a distinguished former lab member's research on scaling [multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL)](/marl) in large agent populations has been published in **Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR)** !

> {% reference subramanian2026tmlr %}


In this paper, Sriram summarized and expanded on results addressing a fundamental limitation in traditional Mean Field RL, the assumptions that agents within a neighborhood are homogeneous and that agents outside have no influence {% cite subramanian2026tmlr %}.

[Dr. Sriram Ganapathi Subramanian]({{site.data.coauthors["sriramganapathisubramanian"].url }}) is a former PhD student in the lab, now an Assistant Professor at Carleton University.
This publication is a culmination, and extension, of the fantastic work Sriram carried out in and since his PhD thesis {% cite subramanian2022uwspace -f theses %}, developing **Mean Field Attention (MFA)**, a novel framework combining attention mechanisms for fine-grained local interactions with mean field approximations for global population dynamics. Sriram showed that MFA consistently outperforms prior mean field baselines across both competitive and cooperative multi-agent benchmarks.
